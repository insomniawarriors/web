### Page Header

- Section label: Clinical CBT-I Treatment
- Heading: The Program

### Program Components

- Section label: What We Provide
- Heading: Program Components
- Description: A comprehensive, multi-component treatment program — not coaching, not tips, not suggestions.
- Component 1 title: Clinical Insomnia Assessment
- Component 1 text: A structured evaluation using validated tools (ISI, sleep diaries, clinical interview and more) to determine insomnia subtype, severity, and contributing factors.
- Component 2 title: CBT-I Treatment Protocol
- Component 2 text: A stepwise, evidence-based intervention including stimulus control, sleep scheduling, cognitive reframing (building healthier sleep thinking patterns and shifting thoughts that keep you awake), and behavioral modification.
- Component 3 title: Co-Occurring Condition Integration
- Component 3 text: Treatment is adapted for individuals with anxiety, PTSD, depression, chronic pain, hormonal changes, long COVID, and other medical contributors. Collaboration with your therapist, psychiatrist, or physician is integrated for most individuals.
- Footnote: This is not coaching. It is a clinical protocol requiring commitment, adherence, and ongoing evaluation. Most individuals participate in a minimum of seven sessions. Many require longer programs depending on the complexity of their insomnia, how their body responds to the therapy, and related co-occurring conditions.

### How The Program Works

- Section label: Step by Step
- Heading: How The Program Works
- Step 1 title: Complete the Clinical Assessment
- Step 1 text: We evaluate your sleep history, symptoms, medical factors, and co-occurring conditions using validated clinical tools.
- Step 2 title: Begin Your Sleep Diaries
- Step 2 text: Once determined eligible, you complete 14 days of sleep diaries before your next session. Daily sleep diaries are mandatory — they are part of what makes this an evidence-based program. You'll track your sleep changes throughout the entire program.
- Step 3 title: Receive a Clinical Treatment Plan
- Step 3 text: Your provider identifies the mechanisms driving your insomnia and outlines a structured CBT-I-based protocol tailored to your unique situation.
- Step 4 title: Engage in Weekly Treatment Sessions
- Step 4 text: Sessions include progress review, data-driven adjustments, and integration of strategies for anxiety, trauma, or medical contributors. The average session commitment is 7–11 sessions.
- Step 5 title: Achieve Sustainable, Natural Sleep
- Step 5 text: With adherence, most individuals experience measurable improvements in sleep efficiency, nighttime awakenings, and confidence in their ability to sleep.

### Note About Underlying Conditions

- Heading: A Note About Underlying Conditions
- Paragraph 1: If certain conditions are identified during the initial intake paperwork, we will recommend a PCP consult first to resolve or begin treating underlying conditions.
- Paragraph 2: Certain conditions like unaddressed sleep apnea may impact the team's ability to effectively treat chronic insomnia. Sleep apnea affects someone's ability to receive enough oxygen when they sleep, and lasting improvement doesn't happen when someone doesn't get enough oxygen while sleeping.
- Paragraph 3: We explore all underlying conditions so that your treatment is whole-person focused and comprehensive — addressing all reasons for your insomnia and your poor night's sleep.

### CBT-I Treats the Root Cause

- Section label: Evidence-Based
- Heading: CBT-I Treats the Root Cause
- Description: CBT-I focuses on the underlying thoughts and behaviors that keep insomnia going — rather than masking the problem with medication. It helps retrain the body's natural sleep system.

- Card 1 title: Proven to Improve Sleep
- Card 1 text: Research shows CBT-I consistently improves: time to fall asleep, nighttime awakenings, overall sleep quality, daytime functioning.

- Card 2 title: No Side Effects
- Card 2 text: CBT-I achieves results superior to sleep medications, with: no dependency, no tolerance buildup, no cognitive or next-day side effects.

- Card 3 title: Long-Lasting Results
- Card 3 text: Unlike medications, CBT-I teaches skills that continue to work after treatment ends. Many patients maintain or even improve their sleep over time.

- Card 4 title: Structured & Practical

## Page: FAQs

### Page Header

- Section label: Common Questions
- Heading: FAQs
- Subtitle: Your questions, answered

### Question: What does a typical session look like?

Each session begins with a review of your sleep diary. This allows us to calculate your sleep efficiency. The remainder of the session focuses on determining if your sleep schedule needs to change, and addressing any other co-occurring conditions that need to be improved for sleep to thrive.

### Question: How do I receive my sessions?

All visits are Telehealth through a medically and HIPAA secure platform. 

### Questions: How long are sessions?

Sessions are typically 60 minutes in length. Towards the end of the program, it may be possible to have visits as short as 15-20 minutes.

### Question: What is the cost?

Prices are $80 for 10-30 minute sessions and $150 for 31-60 minute sessions. 

### Question: How long does treatment take?

Most individuals require a minimum of seven sessions, with many needing up to 11 sessions, depending on medical or psychological contributors.

### Question: What makes you different?

We combine a thoughtful, human-centered approach with clear communication and reliable results. It's not just what we do — it's how we do it that sets us apart.

### Question: Is this therapy?

Yes, this is evidence-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia. This is not sleep coaching or a list of suggestions to maybe improve your sleep. It works for 95% of individuals who adhere to the program and complete it.

### Question: Do you treat co-occurring conditions?

Yes. Insomnia Warriors specializes in cases involving anxiety, PTSD, depression, chronic pain, and other medical contributors.

### Question: Do you accept HSA/FSA or provide superbills?

Yes. Sessions may be eligible for reimbursement depending on your plan. We provide documentation upon request. We do not take medical insurance or bill medical insurance companies as part of this program.

### Question: Why is a sleep diary critical?

Every session starts with a review of your sleep diary. This provides us evidence of whether we have addressed all the components of your insomnia.

When we are missing a component of your insomnia, we will plateau and your sleep improvement will stagnate. This will show in your sleep diary, and is why daily sleep diaries are so integral to this program. Every person's CBT-I therapy will look different based on their unique makeup.

### Question: Do you work with people who take sleep medication?

A close coordination with medical providers will be critical if you are taking sleep medications to determine if the sleep medication is for sleep only or for treating an underlying medical condition. If the medications are for sleeping only, we will work with your therapist, psychiatrist, or physician to wean you off your sleep medications so that your sleep is achieved through natural body processes. You will learn how to do this through this program.

### Question: What if I have tried everything?

We will discuss all the things you have tried in the past and determine which pieces were missing, or which things needed to be tried in parallel that were potentially missed.
