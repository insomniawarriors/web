Insomnia is a treatable condition

Without drugs, just behavioral therapy

Occupational Therapy driven Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (OT-driven CBT-I) is a program for individuals experiencing chronic insomnia and co-occurring conditions such as anxiety, PTSD, depression, chronic pain, and other medical contributors.

OT-driven CBT-I goes beyond psychology-led CBT-I by looking beyond the brain at the whole person: routines, roles, environment, nervous-system patterns, pain, stress, daily demands, and the medical factors that can disrupt sleep.

By treating all causes of insomnia, our program provides a clinically guided non-medication pathway to restore natural sleep. Insomnia Warriors provides telehealth for clients in Seattle and throughout Washington State.

Who This Program Is For

You may be a candidate for this OT-driven CBT-I treatment program if any of these describe your experience.

☾

You lie awake for hours despite being tired or exhausted

⏰

You wake frequently during the night and cannot fall back asleep

☼

You wake for the day much earlier than you want

❓

Your mind becomes alert or anxious at bedtime

⚡

You feel foggy, depleted, or that you don't function well in the daytime

⚫

You've tried sleep hygiene, supplements, or apps without lasting improvement

You want a structured, clinically validated method that focuses on you as a whole person — not another short-term fix.
