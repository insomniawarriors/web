The Gold Standard

Why CBT-I

Industry gold standard for treating chronic insomnia.

Evidence-Based

CBT-I Treats the Root Cause

CBT-I focuses on the underlying thoughts and behaviors that keep insomnia going — rather than masking the problem with medication. It helps retrain the body's natural sleep system.

▲ Proven to Improve Sleep

Research shows CBT-I consistently improves:

  • Time to fall asleep
  • Nighttime awakenings
  • Overall sleep quality
  • Daytime functioning

☑ No Side Effects

CBT-I achieves results superior to sleep medications, with:

  • No dependency
  • No tolerance buildup
  • No cognitive or next-day side effects

↻ Long-Lasting Results

Unlike medications, CBT-I teaches skills that continue to work after treatment ends. Many patients maintain or even improve their sleep over time.

⚙ Structured & Practical

CBT-I is typically delivered over 7–10 sessions and includes practical tools such as:

  • Sleep scheduling
  • Stimulus control (retraining bed–sleep association)
  • Cognitive restructuring (reducing sleep anxiety)
  • Relaxation techniques

✨ Improves Overall Well-Being

Beyond sleep, CBT-I can also improve:

  • Mood (anxiety and depression)
  • Energy and focus
  • Quality of life
Whole person focus

What makes Occupational Therapy driven CBT-I different.

The field of OT focuses on all aspects of someone's life. CBT-I delivered by psychiatrists often includes both medications and the CBT-I therapy. OT driven CBT-I is a non-pharmacological program.

★ Core Focus Occupational Therapy (OT)- driven CBT-I

Centers on daily life structure and function- how sleep fits into routines, roles, environments. Insomnia is treated as a disruption in occupations (sleep, work, self-care, leisure). Emphasizes rebuilding sustainable habits and rhythms.

⚙ Intervention Style

Highly contextual and hands-on: redesigning bedtime routines, modifying the sleep environment, aligning sleep with daytime activity patterns, behavioral tools that are tied to real-life performance (work schedules or caregiving demands).

❤ View of the Patient

Sees you as a whole system interacting with your environment. Your provider puts a strong emphasis on your life's functions, independence, and quality of life. Tailors your therapy to real-world barriors (shift work, parenting, or disabilities)

☑ Use of Medication

Non-pharmacological. The focus is on behavioral and environmental changes- allowing you to directly control your sleep, not be dependent on medications to achieve restful and restorative sleep.

✨ Session Experience

Feels more like coaching and lifestyle redesign while still being clinical therapy. Which includes: activity logs beyond merely sleep (food consumption, naps, medications, caffeine intake), habit training and environmental adjustments, collaborative function-oriented sessions with your provider.
